Mission and vision

Built to keep harvest value in farming communities

In Twi, Kora means to preserve and protect. We treat cold storage as basic rural infrastructure so farmers are never forced to sell perishable crops at distress prices.

Mission

Eliminate farm-gate food loss and double smallholder take-home income

We deploy walk-in, solar-powered cold rooms inside Ghanaian farming districts on a GHS 2 per crate daily rental and connect stored produce directly to schools, supermarkets, and agro-processors.

Vision

A food-secure West Africa powered by clean rural cold chains

A regional agricultural system where every harvested crate reaches a kitchen, rural storage runs entirely on off-grid solar energy, and smallholder farming families earn predictable, dignified incomes.

Operating principles
  • Feature-phone USSD access
    Farmers check crate temperature, book space, and receive Mobile Money payouts by dialing *713*88# on any handset.
  • 100% off-grid thermal storage
    Rooftop monocrystalline solar arrays freeze phase-change thermal plates during the day to hold 4°C through the night without diesel.
  • Women-operated village hubs
    Each cold hub is managed by trained local women agro-operators who handle crate sanitization, digital weighing, and buyer dispatch.
  • Direct B2B buyer aggregation
    By pooling graded crates across village hubs, cooperatives negotiate direct supply contracts with urban hotels, schools, and grocers.
What KoraFresh is about

Solving the 48-hour farm-gate price trap

Without cold storage, farmers must accept whatever price middlemen offer before sunset on harvest day. KoraFresh gives cooperatives three weeks of breathing room.

1. Harvest and crate check-in

Sanitized 25 kg stackable crates

Farmers bring freshly picked tomatoes, sweet peppers, leafy greens, or citrus to the village hub. Produce is transferred from rough wooden boxes that bruise fruit into ventilated plastic crates.

2. Pay-as-you-store cooling

GHS 2 per crate per day, zero upfront cost

Inside the 4°C solar chamber, ripening slows immediately. Farmers require no subscription or collateral; storage fees are deducted only when the produce sells.

3. Direct urban offtake

Instant Mobile Money settlement

Every checked-in crate appears on our buyer inventory ledger. Urban buyers reserve lots online, and farmers receive instant Mobile Money payouts upon dispatch.

Putting time and bargaining power back in the farmer's hands

Growing up in Ghana, Haris Issah saw a recurring contradiction across agricultural markets: smallholder farmers work months to cultivate high-value vegetables, yet remain the lowest-paid participants in the food supply chain because they cannot pause spoilage after harvest.

"When a farmer watches half her tomatoes soften in the afternoon heat, she loses school fees and bargaining power at the same time. Cold storage at the village gate turns a 48-hour distress sale into a three-week fair market."

Formulated at Ashesi University, Haris structured KoraFresh as a self-sustaining social enterprise rather than a grant-dependent charity. Each hub's GHS 2 daily crate tariff covers local women operators' salaries and preventive solar maintenance, while returning more than five times the rental cost in preserved crop value to participating farmers.
